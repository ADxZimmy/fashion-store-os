# UAT

## Scenario

In CRM Inbox, paste or submit a WhatsApp/Instagram-style message from a potential client, review it in the channel intake queue, set the follow-up state, and route it into the inquiry pipeline.

## Result

Pending.

## Notes

Passing UAT means the raw channel message remains traceable, the generated inquiry has the expected client/source/request/deadline context, and the queue clearly shows whether the message is new, routed, or archived.
